Understanding Vaginal Dryness and Genitourinary Syndrome of Menopause (GSM)

Many women experience changes in vaginal comfort throughout different stages of life, particularly during perimenopause, menopause and after certain medical treatments. A reduction in oestrogen can affect the health of the vaginal tissues, leading to dryness, irritation and discomfort. These changes are commonly referred to as Genitourinary Syndrome of Menopause (GSM).

GSM is a common condition that may affect everyday comfort, intimate relationships and overall quality of life. Symptoms can develop gradually and may vary from woman to woman. Although commonly associated with menopause, similar symptoms may also occur after childbirth, during breastfeeding or following treatments that affect hormone levels.

Common symptoms of GSM include:

  • Persistent vaginal dryness
  • Burning or irritation
  • Reduced natural lubrication
  • Discomfort or pain during intimacy
  • Vaginal soreness or sensitivity
  • Recurrent urinary symptoms, such as urgency or discomfort

Not every woman experiencing these symptoms requires the same treatment. Following a comprehensive assessment, we will discuss the most appropriate management options for your individual needs. Depending on your symptoms and clinical findings, these may include pelvic health physiotherapy, lifestyle advice, Vaginal Hyaluronic Acid treatment, Vaginal HIFU, PRP for Intimate Health or referral to your GP or menopause specialist where appropriate.

How Does Vaginal Hyaluronic Acid Treatment Work?

Hyaluronic acid is a naturally occurring molecule found throughout the body that helps retain moisture and supports healthy tissue function. As women age or experience hormonal changes, the natural levels of hyaluronic acid within vaginal tissues may reduce, contributing to dryness and discomfort.

Desirial® Vaginal Hyaluronic Acid is carefully injected into specific areas of the vaginal tissues to improve hydration and support tissue quality. The treatment is designed to restore moisture and improve comfort while maintaining the natural characteristics of the tissue.

Unlike hormonal treatments, Vaginal Hyaluronic Acid contains no hormones and may be considered for women seeking a non-hormonal option, following an individual clinical assessment.
