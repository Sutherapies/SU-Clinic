1. About this Privacy Policy

SU Women's Health Physiotherapy and Wellness Clinic is committed to protecting your privacy and keeping your personal information secure.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information when you contact us, book an appointment, attend the clinic, receive treatment, use our website or communicate with us.

We process personal information in accordance with applicable UK data protection legislation, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Who is responsible for your information?

SU Women's Health Physiotherapy and Wellness Clinic is responsible for the personal information we collect and use in connection with our services.

If you have any questions about how your information is used, or wish to exercise your data protection rights, please contact us at info@sutherapies.co.uk or telephone 07395 196639.

3. Information we collect

Depending on the services you use, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details
  • Date of birth
  • Address where required
  • Emergency contact details where appropriate
  • Appointment and booking information
  • Medical and health history
  • Current symptoms and health concerns
  • Information about previous treatment
  • Medication and relevant medical conditions
  • Pregnancy, childbirth and postnatal information where relevant
  • Pelvic health and women's health information where relevant
  • Clinical assessment and examination findings
  • Treatment and rehabilitation records
  • Information relating to your care and progress
  • Communications between you and the clinic
  • Payment and invoice information
  • Information submitted through online forms
  • Information necessary for safeguarding and safe clinical care

Some of the information we collect, particularly information about your health, is special category personal data under UK data protection law and requires additional protection.

4. How we use your information

We use your information where necessary to:

  • Provide assessment, physiotherapy and treatment
  • Provide safe and appropriate clinical care
  • Maintain accurate clinical records
  • Manage and administer appointments
  • Communicate with you about your appointments and treatment
  • Arrange follow-up care
  • Process payments and invoices
  • Respond to enquiries
  • Manage complaints
  • Meet our professional, legal and regulatory obligations
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of our services
  • Protect patients and others where there is a legitimate safety or safeguarding concern

5. Lawful basis for using your information

Under UK data protection law, we must have a lawful basis for collecting and using personal information.

Depending on the purpose, we may rely on:

  • Contract
  • Legal obligation
  • Legitimate interests
  • Vital interests
  • Consent where appropriate

For health information, which is special category data, we also need an additional condition under Article 9 of the UK GDPR. Where applicable to the provision of healthcare, this may include processing necessary for health or social care purposes under Article 9(2)(h), subject to the relevant safeguards.

We do not generally rely on consent as the lawful basis for processing health information necessary to provide direct healthcare.

6. Clinical records

We maintain clinical records relating to your assessment and treatment. These records may include information about your symptoms, medical history, examination findings, clinical reasoning, treatment, advice, progress and referrals or correspondence relevant to your care.

As a private physiotherapy practice, SU Women's Health Physiotherapy and Wellness Clinic is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality, security, retention and appropriate disposal of clinical records.

7. Cliniko

We use Cliniko for appointment booking, appointment management and clinical record keeping. Personal and health information may be entered into Cliniko where necessary to arrange and provide your care.

8. Payments

We use SumUp to process payments where applicable. Payment information is processed through the relevant payment provider. We do not need to retain your full card details as part of your clinical record.

9. Email, telephone and other communications

We may contact you by email, telephone, SMS or other appropriate communication methods regarding appointments, appointment reminders, treatment and follow-up, clinical administration, payments and invoices, your enquiries, and information directly related to services you have requested.

We take reasonable steps to protect confidential information when communicating with you. Please be aware that ordinary email or SMS may not provide the same level of security as a dedicated clinical system.

10. Video consultations

Where a remote consultation is provided, we may use Zoom. Information necessary to arrange and conduct your consultation may be processed through Zoom.

You should participate in remote consultations from a private and secure environment where possible.

We will not record a video consultation unless there is a specific lawful basis and appropriate arrangements for doing so.

11. Website and online bookings

Our website is www.sutherapies.co.uk.

We may collect information when you contact us through our website, submit an enquiry, use online booking, complete an online form, or request information about our services.

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies. Where consent is required, appropriate information and controls will be provided.

12. Marketing and social media

We may use social media platforms, including Instagram and Facebook, to provide general information about women's health, physiotherapy and our services.

We do not publicly share confidential clinical information about patients.

We will not use identifiable patient photographs, testimonials, treatment information or other identifiable patient information for marketing without an appropriate lawful basis and, where required, your specific permission.

Where marketing communications require consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time.

13. Who we may share your information with

We keep your information confidential and only share it where there is an appropriate lawful basis and where sharing is necessary.

  • Healthcare professionals involved in your care
  • Other healthcare providers where appropriate and lawful
  • Cliniko, for appointment and clinical record management
  • SumUp, for payment processing
  • Zoom, where used for remote consultations
  • Professional advisers, insurers or legal advisers where necessary
  • Relevant authorities where we are legally required to provide information
  • Emergency services where necessary to protect someone's vital interests

We do not sell your personal or health information.

14. Confidentiality

We recognise that women's health and pelvic health information can be particularly sensitive. We take appropriate steps to maintain confidentiality and restrict access to personal and clinical information.

Information will only be shared without your permission where there is an appropriate legal or professional basis to do so, for example where required by law or where necessary to protect someone from serious harm.

15. How we protect your information

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, accidental disclosure, unauthorised alteration, destruction and unauthorised use.

We use appropriate passwords, access controls and secure systems where available. Access to clinical information is restricted to people who have a legitimate need to access it.

16. How long we keep your information

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and to meet our professional, legal, regulatory and accounting obligations.

Clinical records

As a physiotherapy practice, we follow the record-keeping principles and retention guidance provided by the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy (CSP).

For adult patients, clinical records will generally be retained for 8 years from the date of last treatment.

For children and young people, clinical records will generally be retained for 8 years after their 18th birthday or until the individual reaches 25 years of age, whichever is later.

Certain types of records or individual circumstances may require information to be retained for longer. We will review our retention requirements and retain records securely for as long as reasonably necessary.

When information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or disposed of.

17. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights under UK data protection law to request access, correction, restriction, objection, deletion where the law allows, data portability where applicable, and withdrawal of consent where we rely on consent.

These rights are subject to certain legal exceptions. For example, we may need to retain clinical records where there is a professional or legal requirement to do so.

If you wish to exercise any of these rights, please contact info@sutherapies.co.uk.

18. Access to your clinical records

You have the right to request access to the personal information and clinical records we hold about you, subject to applicable legal requirements and exemptions.

Please contact us using info@sutherapies.co.uk. We will deal with your request in accordance with applicable data protection law.

19. International data transfers

Some of the technology and service providers we use may process information outside the UK.

Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place as required by applicable UK data protection law.
